The Grand Saharan Circuit

One Road. Ten Miracles.

Marrakech

The Red City — where the caravan awakens

  1. 01

    Marrakech

    The Red City — where the caravan awakens

    The journey ignites in the Red City, where Jemaa el-Fnaa's smoke curls into the evening. We glide toward the Tizi n'Tichka pass as olive valleys fold into ochre cliffs — the first verse of a poem written in sand.

  2. 02

    Ouarzazate

    Gateway of a thousand kasbahs

    The quiet gateway to the Sahara, city of a thousand kasbahs and cinematic legends. Beyond its sun-baked studios, every kilometre now smells of desert.

  3. 03

    Tazenakht

    The carpet road of the Anti-Atlas

    A high-plateau town where Berber hands knot world-famous carpets. We pause for mint tea among the looms as the Anti-Atlas folds into amber.

  4. ★ Our Home Base
    04

    Foum Zguid & Oasis

    A crown of palms between mountain and sand

    Our home. A crown of palms between mountain and sand, where kasbahs guard cool gardens. Here engines rest, camels rise, and your nomadic life begins.

  5. 05

    Zaouiat Sidi Abdenabi

    The sacred hamlet of caravan blessings

    A sacred hamlet at the desert's edge, where marabout walls whisper centuries of caravan blessings. Nomad families welcome you with bread, tea and the etiquette of the sands.

  6. ★ Signature Wonder
    06

    Lake Iriki

    Driving on a fossil mirror

    A surreal drive on a fossil mirror: the great dried lakebed stretches to the edge of the world. You will swear the clouds float beneath your wheels.

  7. ★ Signature Wonder
    07

    Erg Lmahazil

    The untouched, silent nomadic paradise

    An untouched, silent nomadic paradise — a crescent sea of dunes no crowd has ever touched. Here silence is the presence of eternity.

  8. 08

    Erg Chegaga

    The wild golden mountains

    The wilder sister of Morocco's great ergs: sixty kilometres of golden mountains. We climb the highest crest as the sun melts into ember, then violet, then stars.

  9. 09

    M'hamid El Ghizlane

    Where the road surrenders to sand

    The last oasis before the boundless sand — where asphalt surrenders and the dunes begin. The caravan ghosts of Timbuktu still walk the lanes at dusk.

  10. ◆ Grand Finale
    10

    Merzouga

    Dawn over Erg Chebbi

    The grand finale beneath the towering dunes of Erg Chebbi. Dawn rises like a slow drum — the Sahara is not a place you visit, it is a place that enters you.
